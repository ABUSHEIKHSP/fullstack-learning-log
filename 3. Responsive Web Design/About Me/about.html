Just a laid-back ninja trying to enjoy the simple things in life.

I was always a little different from the other shinobi. While others were busy proving how strong they were, I preferred reading my little orange book, arriving late, and pretending I had somewhere important to be.

Being a shinobi taught me plenty of useful things: stay alert, trust your teammates, and never underestimate a good strategy. Over time, I learned that being strong isn't only about winning fights. Sometimes it's about knowing when to relax and enjoy the moment.

So, how did I turn things around?

I started focusing more on the people around me. Teaching the next generation gave me a new challenge and plenty of opportunities to discover just how creative young ninjas can be.

My students were not exactly easy to handle, but watching them grow made every difficult lesson worthwhile. I learned to guide them, trust them, and occasionally let them figure things out the hard way.

What can my journey teach you?

You don't have to take everything so seriously. Work hard when it matters, learn from your mistakes, and remember to leave some room for the things that make you happy.

Life will always throw unexpected challenges your way. My approach is simple: stay curious, keep your friends close, enjoy the little victories, and never forget to make time for yourself. And if you're wondering, yes, I probably am late for something.
